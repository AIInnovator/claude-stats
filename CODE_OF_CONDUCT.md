# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted. That covers issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/AIInnovator). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break this code.
